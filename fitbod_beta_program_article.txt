Beta testing is the second phase of software testing that involves real users validating functionality and usability. As a beta tester, you may experience: Bugs, crashes, or glitches Features that change rapidly or disappear Early access to unreleased tools and updates If you’d prefer a stable experience, we recommend using the regular App Store version of Fitbod instead. iOS Beta Program How to Enroll for iOS To request access, please email beta@fitbod.me with the following details: Subject line: “I’d like to be a Fitbod beta tester for iOS” In your email, include: Your first and last name Your Fitbod account email address A brief explanation of why you’d like to join the beta program (required) 📌 Applications without a name and reason for joining will not be considered. How to Install Fitbod Beta (iOS) After enrollment: You’ll receive a welcome email with TestFlight instructions. Install the TestFlight app from the App Store. Accept the invitation from Apple to join the Fitbod beta. Download the beta version from TestFlight If you already have the App Store version installed, the beta version will replace it , but your data will remain intact. ⚠️ We do not use the “Redeem” option in TestFlight Android Beta Program ❌ Enrollment Closed We’re not currently accepting new Android beta testers. For updates or questions, email android-beta@fitbod.me . How to Install Fitbod Beta (Android) If already enrolled: Open the Google Play Store. Search for Fitbod. Confirm you see “You’re a beta tester”. Download the available build as normal. 📌 Android beta testers must have an active subscription to use the app. How to Report Beta Feedback iOS Please send feedback to beta @fitbod.me . Use Subject line : iOS Beta Feedback Include: Your first name. Your Fitbod account email. iOS version and beta app version. Detailed description of the issue + screenshots/screen recording, if possible. Android Pease send feedback to android-beta@fitbod.me . Use Subject line : Android Beta Feedback Include: Your first name. Your Fitbod account email. Android device model, OS version, and app version. Detailed description of the issue + screenshots/screen recording, if possible. Switching Between Beta and App Store Version (iOS Only) You can switch between the beta app and App Store version at any time. Do not delete the app from your device to avoid losing data. When no beta is live, install the App Store version until the next beta release. When a new beta is available, open TestFlight and install it directly. Additional Notes Enrolling in the Beta Program does not qualify you for a subscription refund. The iOS beta app is free to use, but the Android beta requires an active subscription. Beta participation is voluntary, and your feedback is incredibly valuable to shaping the future of Fitbod!
